# Pet Hair Panic

A pet grooming time management game. Run a busy grooming salon where pets arrive with specific style requests. Use the right tools, work quickly, and keep your furry customers happy!

## How to Play

Pets arrive at your grooming shop with requests for haircuts, dye jobs, and styling. Drag the right tool onto each pet, then ring them up at the register before their patience runs out.

### Controls

| Action | Mouse/Touch | Keyboard |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Select Scissors (Trim) | Click or drag Scissors tool | Press **1** |
| Select Brush (Fluff) | Click or drag Brush tool | Press **2** |
| Select Clippers (Shave) | Click or drag Clippers tool | Press **3** |
| Select Dye Bottle | Click or drag Dye tool | Press **4** |
| Ring up at Register | Click or drag Register tool | Press **5** |
| Apply to pet | Click on pet (after tool selected) | Click on pet |
| Start / Continue | Click "START" or "Play Again" | Enter |

### Service Steps

Each pet needs:

1. **Trim** -- Use the correct fur tool (Scissors ✂️, Brush 🪥, or Clippers ⚡)
2. **Dye** -- If they want color, use the dye bottle with the matching color
3. **Register** -- Ring up the finished pet at the register

### Scoring

- **Base revenue**: $10 per pet
- **Tip bonus**: Bonus based on speed and satisfaction level
- **Combo bonus**: Serve pets quickly for multipliers up to 3x
- **Wrong tool**: Reduces satisfaction, may anger the pet

### Levels

| Level | Name | Pets | Time | Target Revenue |
|-------|------|------|------|----------------|
| 1 | Puppy Start | 3 | 60s | $30 |
| 2 | Cat & Dogs | 5 | 90s | $55 |
| 3 | Full Service | 7 | 120s | $90 |
| 4 | Rush Hour | 9 | 120s | $120 |
| 5 | Grand Opening | 12 | 150s | $170 |

Complete a level to unlock the next one. Earn up to 3 stars per level based on your revenue.

### Tips

- Watch the happiness bar -- green is happy, yellow is warning, red is danger
- Use the right tool for the right pet -- check their speech bubble!
- Build combos by serving pets quickly one after another
- Dye color must match what the pet wants -- check the request icons
- Time your register step carefully at the end!

## Requirements

- Modern browser with ES5+ support
- Touch-enabled devices supported
- Sound requires user interaction to initialize
